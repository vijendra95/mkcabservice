Jaipur to Delhi Taxi — Complete Travel Guide, Fare & Tips

22 August 2026 · MK Cab Service

How far is Delhi from Jaipur?

The Jaipur to Delhi distance is about 268 km via NH48, one of the best-maintained expressway-grade highways in India. A comfortable cab journey takes around 5 to 5.5 hours including a tea break.

What does a Jaipur to Delhi taxi cost?

With MK Cab Service, one-way sedan fare is a fixed ₹4,000 for a one-way drop. SUVs and Innova Crysta are available for families and groups. There are no hidden charges: tolls, state tax and parking are billed at actuals.

Best stops on the way

Popular breaks on NH48 include Behror Midway (roughly halfway) for food and clean washrooms, and Neemrana Fort if you have time for a quick detour. Your driver will know the best current options.

Booking tips

  • Book a few hours ahead for early-morning departures.
  • Share your Delhi drop point (airport terminal, Gurugram, Noida etc.) for an exact quote.
  • One-way trips are charged one side only — no return fare.
