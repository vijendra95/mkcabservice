Best Places to Visit in Rajasthan by Car — Routes from Jaipur

22 August 2026 · MK Cab Service

Udaipur — the city of lakes (393 km, ~7h)

Palaces, lakes and rooftop cafes. The drive via NH48/NH58 is smooth, with Chittorgarh Fort making a great stop on the way.

Jodhpur — the blue city (337 km, ~6h)

Mehrangarh Fort towers over blue-washed lanes. Combine it with Jaisalmer for a full desert circuit.

Jaisalmer — the golden city (558 km, ~9h)

Sandstone havelis, the living fort and Sam sand dunes. Best done as a multi-day round trip with an overnight stop.

Ranthambore — tiger country (160 km, ~3h)

One of India's best tiger reserves, an easy weekend trip from Jaipur. Book safari slots in advance.

Mount Abu — Rajasthan's only hill station (485 km, ~8h)

Cool air, Nakki Lake and the Dilwara temples — a refreshing break from the plains.
