1. Choose the right car for your group

1–4 travellers with light luggage? A sedan is the most economical. Family with bags — pick an SUV or Innova Crysta. Groups of 9 or more travel best in a Tempo Traveller.

2. Understand the per-km fare

A genuine quote covers the car, fuel and driver. Driver allowance should be shown separately, and tolls or state tax billed at actuals — never accept vague "all included" numbers without a breakup.

3. One-way vs round trip

If you're not coming back the same way, book a one-way cab — you pay only one side. Round trips are billed both ways with a minimum of 250 km/day.

4. Confirm the fare before you board

A reliable operator confirms the full estimated fare in writing (WhatsApp works great) before the trip — and sticks to it.

5. Ask about the driver

Verified, experienced highway drivers make a big difference on long routes, especially for night driving.

6. Book ahead in peak season

October to March is Rajasthan's tourist season — booking a day ahead gets you a better car at the same price.

7. Keep support on WhatsApp

Choose a service that answers on WhatsApp 24x7 — so plan changes, extra stops or delays are sorted in one message.
